# LoadingSpinner

## Purpose
Centered spinner for full-page loading states. Replaces skeleton pulse animations.

## Props
None

## Behaviour
- Centered vertically and horizontally
- Classic rotating border spinner
- 48px diameter
- Minimum height of 200px to ensure proper centering on small screens

## Used by
- app/page.tsx
- app/history/page.tsx
- app/history/[sessionId]/page.tsx
- app/session/[sessionId]/page.tsx
